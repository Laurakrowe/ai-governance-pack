**AI Policy Package \-- Reader\'s Guide**

**Companion to:** Artificial Intelligence (AI) Policy and the full AI governance document set

**Audience:** Organization leadership, IT, managers, and staff

**Prepared by:** Laura Rowe

This package contains the Organization\'s AI Policy and the supporting documents that put it into practice. This guide is the map: it explains, in one place, what each document is, who it is for, and when to reach for it. Start here, then route each document to the right audience.

**[Start Here \-- Rollout Sequence]{.underline}**

1. **Adopt the policy.** Leadership reviews and formally adopts the AI Policy, deciding the one open item raised in the cover letter (whether staff must disclose AI use on public-facing materials).

2. **Reach all staff.** Once the policy is adopted, distribute the Acceptable Use Guide and the Copilot FAQ and Tips to all staff. These are the everyday, plain-language pieces.

3. **Brief the managers.** Give every manager and director the AI Governance Quick Reference and the Vendor AI Evaluation Cheat Sheet, and point them to the Change Request form for any new AI tool.

4. **Equip IT.** IT retains the AI Monitoring and Administration Guide, the Technical Controls Appendix, and the AI FactSheets, and keeps the Change Request and Incident Reporting templates ready to use.

5. **Route to Legal.** Send the Legal Review Memo to Legal Counsel for the two pending tools (Wealth Engine and ChatGPT); record the determinations on the AI FactSheets when they return.

**[Who Reads What]{.underline}**

**All staff**

- **Copilot FAQ and Tips** \-- how to use Microsoft Copilot and Copilot Chat day to day, and what is and isn\'t available. Post it where staff can find it.

- **AI Acceptable Use Guide** \-- the plain-language rules for using AI at work. Distribute to all staff; the approved-tools list mirrors the policy.

**Managers and Directors**

- **AI Governance Quick Reference** \-- the one-stop manager reference: the three rules, when to loop in IT, the approval workflow, and a quick decision flow. Give it to every manager.

- **Vendor AI Evaluation Cheat Sheet** \-- use it in any vendor meeting where the product may involve AI. The questions to ask and how to score the answers.

- **Change Request \-- Sanctioned AI Tool** \-- the form a Director submits to request adding a new AI tool to the approved list.

**IT**

- **AI Monitoring and Administration Guide** \-- the IT runbook for the monitoring stack: where to find things, routine checks, and what is and isn\'t enforced.

- **AI Policy Technical Controls Appendix** \-- the control-to-policy map and the Microsoft 365 GCC constraints behind the policy. Internal IT reference.

- **AI FactSheets (Canva, Placer, FreshService)** \-- the per-tool risk records. IT maintains these and completes a new one for each tool that goes through review.

- **AI Incident Reporting Template** \-- intake form for AI-related incidents; routes to the IT Manager.

**Leadership**

- **AI Policy** \-- the policy to review, finalize, and formally adopt. The authoritative document the rest of the package supports.

- **AI Policy Appendices** \-- recommended templates and outlines (AI FactSheet, Incident Response Plan, Governance Manual) the Organization can adopt over time.

- **Cover Letter** \-- the transmittal: what changed in this version and the decision requested of leadership.

**Legal Counsel**

- **Legal Review Memo** \-- focused questions for Legal on the two high-risk tools (Wealth Engine and ChatGPT) before they can be sanctioned.

**[Where Do I Go For\...]{.underline}**

- **"A staff member wants to use a new AI tool."** The Director submits the Change Request \-- Sanctioned AI Tool form to the IT Manager. No use begins before it is approved.

- **"Someone may have pasted sensitive data into an AI tool."** Use the AI Incident Reporting Template and notify the IT Manager. Do not try to undo or hide it.

- **"A vendor\'s product may use AI."** Work through the Vendor AI Evaluation Cheat Sheet before committing, and forward the answers to IT.

- **"Is this tool approved?"** Check the approved-tools list in AI Policy Section 11, mirrored in the Acceptable Use Guide.

- **"How does Copilot work and what can I use?"** The Copilot FAQ and Tips.

- **"What are the rules for using AI at work?"** The AI Acceptable Use Guide, with the full detail in the AI Policy.

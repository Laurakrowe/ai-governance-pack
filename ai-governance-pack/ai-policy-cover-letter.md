**What changed from v0.2**

The following changes were incorporated based on direction from the IT Manager and the advisory firm team following the June leadership review.

- **Scope expanded to all Organization AI tools** (Sections 1, 2). The policy now explicitly covers three categories of AI: standalone generative AI tools, embedded AI features in approved business applications, and high-risk decision-support AI. This addresses the request to go beyond chat AI.

- **the affiliated Foundation integrated** (Sections 1, 2, 4, 6.1, 6.2, 9, 12). Foundation personnel operating on Organization payroll and using the Organization\'s M365 tenant are in scope. The Foundation signs its own contracts and Data Processing Agreements for Foundation-procured tools as a separate legal entity; the IT Manager reviews any such tool used on Organization infrastructure or with Organization data.

- **Consumer AI tool ban added** (Section 6.3). Free, personal, and consumer-tier generative AI tools are prohibited for Organization and Foundation business, including personal-account use of any AI service for Organization work. The prohibition is scoped to unsanctioned consumer chatbots \-- it does not affect Copilot Chat or any tool on the sanctioned list.

- **AI Tool Approval Workflow added** (Sections 5.1, 5.2). A two-gate Director to IT Manager process for all new AI tool requests, including free trials and personal-account use. High-risk tools are routed to Legal Counsel before sanctioning.

- **Sanctioned-tools list expanded** (Section 11). Thirteen tools are now listed with tier, conditions, and notes \-- the Organization\'s Microsoft AI tools plus the embedded and Foundation tools. Two tools (Wealth Engine, ChatGPT) sit in a separate "In AI Review \-- Pending Legal" category, neither sanctioned nor prohibited until Legal Counsel returns a determination.

- **Four policy additions** from the Foundation tools evaluation: AI artifacts as public records (Section 8), external sharing of PII to AI tools / data-broker exposure (Section 6.1), transparency-in-practice for AI meeting note-taking (Section 3.4), and expanded Confidential data definitions for donor/prospect profiles and minors\' records (Sections 3.3, 6.1).

- **New definitions** (Section 12): Embedded AI, high-risk AI, AI artifact, consumer AI tool, the affiliated Foundation, AI Review, and AI Tool Approval Workflow.

- **AI Acceptable Use Guide retained** as the employee-facing companion. The plain-language guide is kept as the staff-facing companion to the policy; its approved-tools list (Section 5) is reconciled to mirror the policy\'s authoritative Section 11 sanctioned-tools list, so the two stay in step.

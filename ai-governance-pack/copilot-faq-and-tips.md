**Microsoft 365 Copilot FAQ and Tips**

**Audience:** All Organization staff

***Internal use only.** Last updated: 2026-06-01*

**[FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS]{.underline}**

**What is Microsoft 365 Copilot?**

Microsoft 365 Copilot is an AI assistant built into the Microsoft 365 apps you already use - Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook. It can draft text, summarize documents, analyze data, and help you work faster. Copilot uses your organization\'s data securely within the Organization\'s Microsoft 365 environment.

Microsoft 365 Copilot requires a dedicated license. If you do not have a Copilot license, see the Copilot Chat section below for what is available to you.

**What is Copilot Chat?**

Copilot Chat is a free AI chat tool available to all Organization staff at copilot.microsoft.com. It works like a general-purpose AI assistant for brainstorming, drafting, answering questions, and research. Copilot Chat does not access your Organization files, email, or calendar - it responds based on its general training.

In the Organization\'s environment, Copilot Chat does not search the live web by default. Treat it as a writing and reasoning aid rather than a current-events search engine, and verify any facts that depend on recent information.

Copilot Chat is accessed through your web browser only. It is not available inside desktop apps (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook) for unlicensed users.

**What AI tools am I allowed to use?**

The Organization has approved the following AI tools for work use:

- Microsoft 365 Copilot (licensed users)

- Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat (all staff)

All other AI tools - including ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and similar consumer AI services - are not approved for Organization work. The Organization monitors AI tool usage through browser extensions installed on managed devices.

If you need an AI tool that is not currently approved, contact your supervisor or IT.

**What should I NOT do with AI tools?**

- Do not enter personally identifiable information (PII) - such as Social Security numbers, medical records, financial account numbers, or employee personal details - into any AI tool, including Copilot

- Do not use AI-generated content as a final product without reviewing it first

- Do not use personal AI subscriptions or accounts for Organization work

- Do not attempt to bypass or disable the browser monitoring extensions

- Do not rely on AI for legal, medical, or financial decisions without professional review

**How do I access Copilot Chat?**

- Open your browser on an Organization device

- Go to copilot.microsoft.com

- Sign in with your Organization Microsoft 365 account

- Start a conversation

This works for all Organization staff, whether or not you have a Copilot license.

**How do I use Copilot in Word, Excel, or Outlook?**

These features are only available if you have a Copilot license. If you do not see the Copilot icon in your toolbar ribbon, you do not have a license assigned. Use Copilot Chat at copilot.microsoft.com instead.

If you have a Copilot license, look for the Copilot icon in the bottom right corner of your M365 apps. Click it to open the Copilot panel.

- **Word:** Ask Copilot to draft, rewrite, summarize, or adjust the tone of text

- **Excel:** Ask Copilot to analyze data, create formulas, highlight trends, or generate charts

- **Outlook:** Ask Copilot to draft replies, summarize email threads, or adjust message tone

- **PowerPoint:** Ask Copilot to create slides from a topic or document, or redesign existing slides

**[TIPS FOR GETTING BETTER RESULTS]{.underline}**

**Be specific**

**Instead of:** "Write something about our summer programs." **Try:** "Draft a 200-word email to parents announcing the 2026 summer recreation program registration dates, including the early bird deadline."

**Give context**

**Instead of:** "Summarize this." **Try:** "Summarize this 10-page report into 5 bullet points for the Board of Directors, focusing on budget impact and timeline."

**Reference your files (licensed users)**

In Word, PowerPoint, and other M365 apps, type "/" to reference a specific file by name. This grounds Copilot\'s output in your actual data instead of generating from scratch. **Try:** "Summarize /Q1 Budget Report.xlsx in three bullet points for the finance committee." **Or:** "Create a presentation based on /Summer Programs Overview.docx."

**Set the format**

**Instead of:** "Help me with this data." **Try:** "Create a table comparing last year\'s park attendance by month with this year\'s numbers, and highlight months where attendance dropped more than 10%."

**Iterate**

Your first prompt will rarely be perfect. Follow up with refinements:

- "Make this shorter."

- "Use a more formal tone."

- "Add a section about safety guidelines."

- "Rewrite the second paragraph to focus on cost savings."

**Tell Copilot what role to play**

**Try:** "You are a communications specialist for a public parks agency. Draft a press release about our new accessible playground."

**Point Copilot at a specific file or person with "/"**

In the licensed Copilot apps, type a forward slash ("/") in the Copilot prompt box to pull a specific item into your request: a file, an email, a person, or a meeting - instead of describing it. **Try:** "Summarize /Budget FY2026 and list the three largest line-item changes from last year." **Or:** "Draft a reply to the latest email from John Doe confirming the registration deadline." Start typing the name after the slash and Copilot will suggest matches you already have access to.

**Manage long conversations**

Copilot Chat starts fresh with each new conversation. It does not remember previous sessions and can also lose track of details in a long conversation.

To get the best results:

- Start a new chat for each distinct topic or task rather than continuing one long thread.

- Keep individual prompts focused on one task at a time.

- If a conversation is lengthy and you don\'t want to lose context, ask Copilot: "Write a handoff summary of our conversation so far. Include the key decisions we made, any constraints or requirements given, and where we left off." Paste that summary as the opening message in a new chat so Copilot has the context it needs to pick up where you left off.

**[COMMON QUESTIONS]{.underline}**

**Q: Is Copilot reading all my files?**

**A:** Copilot can only access files and data you already have permission to see in Microsoft 365. It follows the same access controls as your regular M365 account. It does not access files you do not have permission to view. If you have questions about what files and folders you have access to, contact IT.

**Q: Can I trust what Copilot generates?**

**A:** Copilot is a drafting and reasoning aid, not an authoritative source. Always review AI-generated content for accuracy before sending, publishing, or acting on it. AI can produce plausible-sounding but incorrect information.

**Q: Does Copilot Chat access my email or files?**

**A:** No. Copilot Chat (at copilot.microsoft.com) does not access your Organization email, files, calendar, or any other organizational data. Only the licensed Copilot experience embedded in Word, Excel, Outlook, and PowerPoint accesses your organizational data.

**Q: I don\'t have a Copilot license. What can I use?**

**A:** All Organization staff can use Copilot Chat at copilot.microsoft.com in your browser. This gives you a general-purpose AI assistant for brainstorming, drafting, research, and answering questions. It does not access your Organization files or email. If you need the full Copilot experience in your desktop apps, talk to your supervisor about whether a license is appropriate for your role.

**Q: Can I use Copilot on my personal phone or computer?**

**A:** Copilot Chat is accessible from any browser where you can sign in with your Organization account. The full Copilot experience in M365 apps requires the desktop or web versions of those apps on a managed device. The browser monitoring extension only covers Organization-managed devices.

**Q: Why doesn\'t Copilot Chat know about recent news or events?**

**A:** In the Organization\'s environment, Copilot Chat does not search the web for current information by default. It responds based on its training data. For time-sensitive topics, verify information through other sources.

**Q: Will Copilot remember what I asked it yesterday?**

**A:** No. Each Copilot Chat conversation starts fresh. Copilot does not retain information from previous sessions. If you need to continue work from an earlier conversation, use the handoff technique described in the tips section above.

**Q: What is the browser extension on my computer?**

**A:** The Microsoft Purview browser extension is installed on all Organization-managed Windows devices across Edge, Chrome, and Firefox. It allows the Organization\'s IT team to monitor AI tool usage patterns - which AI tools are being used, how often, and whether sensitive data is being shared with those tools. The extension runs quietly in the background and does not block access to any websites. You cannot and should not attempt to remove or disable it.

**Q: What happens if I visit an unapproved AI website?**

**A:** The Organization monitors AI tool usage on managed devices but does not currently block access to AI websites. Visits to unapproved tools are recorded - third-party AI site visits surface in Cloud Discovery, and any sensitive data shared with those sites surfaces in Purview Activity Explorer. Unapproved tools (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and similar consumer AI services) should not be used for Organization work - use Copilot Chat or your licensed Copilot apps instead. If you believe a tool should be approved for your work, contact your supervisor or IT.

**Q: What happens if I accidentally paste sensitive information into an AI tool?**

**A:** Notify your supervisor and IT immediately. Do not attempt to "undo" or hide the action. The Organization\'s monitoring tools may flag the interaction automatically.

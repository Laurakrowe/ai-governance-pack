**AI Governance Quick Reference**

**Audience:** Managers and above (Directors, GM Office, Foundation Director of Strategic Partnerships and Philanthropy)

**Companion document:** Vendor AI Evaluation Cheat Sheet (for in-depth vendor reviews)

**Owner:** IT Manager

**Version:** Draft v0.1

**[WHAT THIS GUIDE IS]{.underline}**

A short, practical reference for managers to handle day-to-day AI questions without needing to call IT every time. Covers basic AI safety, compliance, data storage, and the questions to ask when a vendor\'s product may use AI behind the scenes.

For deeper vendor evaluations, use the Vendor AI Evaluation Cheat Sheet. For the full policy, see the Organization AI Policy.

**[THE THREE RULES]{.underline}**

- **Sanctioned tools only.** If a tool isn\'t on the sanctioned list, it isn\'t approved for Organization or Foundation use. The authoritative sanctioned-tools list is maintained in AI Policy Section 11; the Acceptable Use Guide carries a plain-language mirror for staff.

- **No free or consumer AI chatbots.** Personal ChatGPT, free Gemini, free Claude, and similar consumer-grade AI services are prohibited for Organization and Foundation work. See AI Policy Section 6.3.

- **No Confidential data in unsanctioned tools.** Donor records, financial data, personnel information, volunteer PII, and anything classified Confidential per the Cyber Security Policy stays out of any AI tool that hasn\'t been formally sanctioned.

**[WHEN TO LOOP IN IT]{.underline}**

Contact the IT Manager when:

- A staff member or department wants to purchase or start a free trial of a new AI tool

- A vendor mentions AI features in their product (whether existing or new)

- A tool you already use adds AI features in an update

- You suspect Confidential data may have been entered into an unsanctioned AI tool

- A vendor cannot answer basic questions about how their AI handles Organization data

- Staff ask whether a specific tool is sanctioned

You do not need to contact IT for:

- Using sanctioned tools for their approved purpose

- Asking general questions about how AI works

- Reviewing this guide or sharing it with your team

**[THE APPROVAL WORKFLOW]{.underline}**

Before any AI tool is purchased, trialed, or used with Organization or Foundation data:

- **Step 1.** Department Director (or equivalent manager) reviews the request against this guide and the AI Policy.

- **Step 2.** Director submits a change request to the IT Manager using the AI tool change-request form.

- **Step 3.** IT Manager evaluates against policy, completes an AI FactSheet for the tool, and routes to Legal Counsel if Confidential data is involved or the tool is high-risk.

- **Step 4.** IT Manager issues a sanctioning decision (approved, conditional, declined, or pending Legal review).

- **Step 5.** Once sanctioned, the tool is added to the approved list and may be used per its approved scope.

No purchase, trial, or use begins before Step 4 is complete.

**[BASIC AI SAFETY CONCERNS]{.underline}**

- **Treat AI output as a draft, not a deliverable.** AI can produce confident-sounding errors. Human review is required before any AI-generated content is sent externally, published, or used to make a decision.

- **Do not enter Confidential data into AI tools.** Even sanctioned tools have data-handling boundaries. If you wouldn\'t email it to an outside party without protections, don\'t paste it into an AI tool.

- **Assume anything entered into a consumer AI tool may be retained or used to train future models.** Enterprise-tier tools with proper agreements behave differently. Consumer tiers do not.

- **AI cannot make decisions on the Organization\'s behalf.** AI may inform, draft, or summarize. People make the decisions.

- **Disclose AI-assisted note-taking.** If a meeting tool (Zoom, Teams, etc.) is taking AI-generated notes or summaries, participants must be informed before the meeting begins. See AI Policy Section 3.4.

**[STORAGE AND DATA HANDLING]{.underline}**

- AI tools may retain prompts, conversations, and outputs. Retention varies by tool and tier.

- Prompts, chat histories, and AI-generated summaries may be public records subject to disclosure under CPRA. Treat them with the same care as email.

- Records retention schedules apply to AI-generated content the same way they apply to other Organization records.

- Sanctioned enterprise-tier tools generally store data within the Organization\'s tenant or under contractual protection. Consumer-tier tools do not.

**[VENDOR AND THIRD-PARTY AI QUESTIONS]{.underline}**

When a vendor pitches a product, or when an existing vendor adds AI features, ask:

- **Is AI used in this product?** If unclear, the answer is probably yes \-- most modern SaaS has AI somewhere in the stack.

- **What Organization data does the AI touch?** Distinguish between data Organization provides to the vendor vs. data the vendor analyzes on the back end.

- **Will Organization\'s data be used to train the vendor\'s models?** The acceptable answer is no, with that commitment in the contract.

- **Where is the AI processing happening?** On the vendor\'s infrastructure, a sub-processor like OpenAI or Anthropic, or somewhere else?

- **Is the AI feature on by default, or can it be disabled?** Default-on AI features in tools Organization already uses are the most common surprise.

- **Does the vendor have a Data Processing Agreement that covers AI?** Standard DPAs may not address AI training, retention, or sub-processor flow.

If a vendor cannot answer these questions clearly, escalate to IT. Use the Vendor AI Evaluation Cheat Sheet for a structured walk-through.

**[QUICK DECISION FLOW]{.underline}**

A staff member wants to use an AI tool. Ask:

- Is it on the sanctioned list (AI Policy Section 11 / Acceptable Use Guide)? If yes: proceed within its approved scope. If no: continue.

- Is it a free or consumer AI chatbot? If yes: prohibited per Section 6.3. If no: continue.

- Will it touch any Confidential data? If yes: submit a change request before any use. If no: continue.

- Is the cost under the relevant approval threshold and the tool\'s AI features minor? Submit a change request anyway. All AI tools route through the workflow.

When in doubt, ask IT.

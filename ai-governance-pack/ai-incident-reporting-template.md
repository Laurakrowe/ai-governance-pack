**[ROUTING]{.underline}**

- For Foundation-related incidents, the IT Manager coordinates with the Foundation Director of Strategic Partnerships and Philanthropy.

**[PROCESS NOTES]{.underline}**

- This template covers AI-specific incidents. General IT security incidents follow existing the Organization's IT incident response procedures under the Cyber Security Policy.

- Per the AI Policy (Section 6.2), if staff become aware of an instance where an AI system has caused harm, staff must report the instance to their supervisor and the IT Manager.

- All incidents will be reviewed to determine whether the AI tool\'s sanctioned status, monitoring configuration, or acceptable-use guidance should be updated.

**[PROCESS NOTES]{.underline}**

- All AI tools not on the sanctioned list are classified as unsanctioned per the AI Policy. Staff must not use unsanctioned tools for Organization work until this change request is approved through the AI Tool Approval Workflow (Section 5.1).

- Approved tools are tagged Sanctioned in the Defender for Cloud Apps Cloud App Catalog and excluded from any future endpoint blocking policies.

- The IT security & AI Review includes checking the tool\'s entry in the MDA Cloud App Catalog for risk score, compliance certifications, and data-handling practices, and routing high-risk tools to Legal Counsel per Section 5.2.

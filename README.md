# AI Governance Policy Pack (Microsoft 365 / Copilot)

A complete, adoptable AI governance package for a regulated Microsoft 365 environment. It covers the policy itself plus everything needed to put it into practice: plain-language staff guidance, a manager decision workflow, IT controls and monitoring, vendor-AI evaluation, incident reporting, and a legal-review path for high-risk tools.

This pack was built during a consulting engagement for a mid-sized California public-sector agency operating in Microsoft 365 GCC, then stripped of all client-identifying detail and generalized for reuse. The core policy is adapted from the publicly available GovAI Coalition AI Policy Template.

Authored by Laura Rowe.

## Who it is for

Organizations standing up AI governance in Microsoft 365, especially regulated or public-sector environments adopting Copilot that need to govern it without stalling adoption.

## Start here

Read `ai-policy-package-readers-guide.md` first. It maps every document to its audience and says when to reach for each one. The rollout sequence it lays out: adopt the policy, reach all staff, brief the managers, equip IT, then route high-risk tools to Legal.

## What is inside

**Core**
- `ai-policy.md`: the authoritative AI Policy: scope, roles, data handling, the sanctioned-tool list, and the AI review and approval workflow.

**All staff**
- `acceptable-use.md`: plain-language rules for using AI at work.
- `copilot-faq-and-tips.md`: day-to-day Copilot usage and what is and is not available.

**Managers**
- `ai-governance-handbook.md`: a one-page manager quick reference and decision flow.
- `vendor-ai-evaluation-cheat-sheet.md`: what to ask when a vendor product may involve AI.
- `change-request-sanctioned-ai-tool.md`: the form to request adding a new AI tool.

**IT**
- `ai-monitoring-and-administration-guide.md`: the monitoring and administration runbook.
- `ai-policy-technical-appendix.md`: control-to-policy mapping and Microsoft 365 GCC constraints.
- `ai-incident-reporting-template.md`: intake form for AI-related incidents.
- `factsheet-placer-ai.md`: an example per-tool risk record (AI FactSheet).

**Leadership and Legal**
- `ai-policy-appendices.md`: templates and outlines (FactSheet, incident response plan, governance manual).
- `ai-policy-cover-letter.md`: a transmittal template for leadership adoption.
- `legal-review-memo.md`: an example legal-review framing for high-risk tools, covering data-broker exposure and tier-based SaaS AI risk.

## How to adapt it

1. Replace "the Organization" with your entity, and set your own data-classification terms.
2. Edit the sanctioned and unsanctioned tool lists in the policy (Section 11) to match your environment.
3. Assign the roles (IT Manager, Legal Counsel, and so on) to your actual owners.
4. Route any high-risk tools (profiling, data brokers, consumer-tier AI) through the legal-review memo before sanctioning.

## Notes

- Adapted from the GovAI Coalition AI Policy Template, which is publicly available.
- Example tool names (Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, Placer.ai, Canva, FreshService) are real products used to illustrate evaluation. They are not endorsements.
- This is governance tooling, not legal advice. Have counsel review before adoption.
